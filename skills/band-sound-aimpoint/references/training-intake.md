# Training Intake

Outside learning helps, but it has different value than user calibration.

## High-Value Internet Or Training Material

Most useful:

- Before/after mix breakdowns where the engineer explains why each change was made.
- Full worship multitrack walkthroughs with section decisions, not only plugin presets.
- Vocal-in-context examples, especially lead vocal against piano/acoustic/electric guitars.
- Bus processing examples for drums, band, vocals, livestream, and mono church deployment.
- Live sound examples that discuss feedback, stage bleed, latency, and mono compatibility.
- Captioned videos where concepts can be distilled into diagnosis rules without storing transcript text.
- Preset/session files that reveal actual chain order and bus strategy.

Less useful by itself:

- Plugin demo videos with isolated sources only.
- Preset-pack marketing without audio context.
- Mastering loudness videos aimed at releases instead of church service mixes.

## What Public Material Can Teach

- Common workflows.
- Source-specific plugin roles.
- Vocabulary for diagnosing mixes.
- Typical worship balance priorities.
- Reasonable starting points for chains.
- Mistakes to avoid.
- Which plugin moves respected engineers reach for first when a source is unstable, masked, harsh, or too dynamic.

## What Requires The User

- Whether the church wants CityAlight naturalness, Bethel polish, or a local hybrid.
- Acceptable amount of vocal tuning.
- Acceptable amount of vocal compression and polish.
- Whether BGVs should feel choir-like, supportive, or mostly tucked.
- How loud drums should feel for the room/congregation.
- Mono deployment expectations.
- Whether a mix feels worshipful to the actual team.

## Best Training Loop

1. Codex makes two or three targeted candidates.
2. User gives a short taste call on one dimension.
3. Codex logs the exact preference and inferred rule.
4. Codex applies the rule to the next candidate.
5. Repeat across different sections and songs.

The fastest calibration comes from repeated small choices, not long general feedback.
